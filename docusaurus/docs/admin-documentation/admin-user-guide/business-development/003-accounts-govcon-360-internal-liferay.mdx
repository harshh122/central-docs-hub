Manage your account profile and point of contact information by navigating to **Accounts → Point of Contact**.

![Accounts](/img/admin/003-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Point of contact details

Complete or update the required contact details for your account setup. Use the progress tracker at the top to navigate between **General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the contact's first name. |
| **Last Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the contact's last name. |
| **Email** | Email | Yes | Enter the contact's email address. |
| **Designation** | Text | No | Enter the contact's job title or role. |
| **Office Number** | Telephone | No | Select your country code and enter the phone number. |
| **Extension** | Text | No | Enter an optional phone extension. |

## Navigation

Use the buttons at the bottom of the form to move through the setup wizard:
- Click **Previous** to return to the previous section.
- Click **Next** to save your progress and advance to the next step.
