Manage and update your organization's legal and entity information by navigating to **Accounts** → **Company Details**.

![Accounts](/img/admin/006-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Company Details

The **Company Details** step allows you to record critical corporate identifiers, registration numbers, and structural classifications. Use the progress tracker at the top to move between **General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the official legal name of your organization. |
| **Legal Structure / Entity Type** | Dropdown | No | Select your corporate entity structure (e.g., LLC, Corporation). |
| **DUNS** | Text | No | Enter your Data Universal Numbering System identifier. |
| **Primary NAICS** | Dropdown | No | Enter or select your primary North American Industry Classification System code. |
| **CAGE Code** | Text | No | Enter your Commercial and Government Entity code. |
| **SIC** | Dropdown | No | Select or type your Standard Industrial Classification code. |

## Managing your record

1. Review and update your information across the multi-step sections (**General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**).
2. Click **Previous** to return to the previous section if you need to make corrections.
3. Click **Save** to preserve your changes.
