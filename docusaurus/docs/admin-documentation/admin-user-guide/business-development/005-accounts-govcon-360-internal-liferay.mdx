Manage your organization's core profile information, identifiers, and company details by navigating to **Accounts** and selecting the **Company Details** section.

![Accounts](/img/admin/005-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Company Details

Use the multi-step form to review and update your organization's entity identifiers and corporate structure.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the official legal name of your organization. |
| **Legal Structure / Entity Type** | Dropdown | No | Select your corporate entity classification. |
| **DUNS** | Text | No | Enter your Data Universal Numbering System identifier. |
| **Primary NAICS** | Dropdown | No | Select or type your primary North American Industry Classification System code. |
| **CAGE Code** | Text | No | Enter your Commercial and Government Entity code. |
| **SIC** | Dropdown | No | Select or type your Standard Industrial Classification code. |

## Managing Form Navigation

1. Click through the progress tracker steps (**General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**) to navigate between setup sections.
2. Use **Previous** to return to the prior setup screen.
3. Click **Save** to preserve your changes.
