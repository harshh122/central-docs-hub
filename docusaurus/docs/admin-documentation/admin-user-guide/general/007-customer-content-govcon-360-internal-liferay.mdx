The Customer Content page allows you to select a client and access their market intelligence and lead prospecting modules. You can reach this page by navigating to **Customer Content** in the main navigation bar.

![Customer Content](/img/admin/007-customer-content-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Client selection

Use the client selector dropdown at the top of the page to switch between different organizations.

| Element | Type | Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Choose the client you want to work with** | Select | No | Search or select the specific client organization whose data and insights you want to view. |

## Market Intel and Lead Prospector

Once you choose a client, the page populates with two main management cards containing quick-access links to core features:

- **Market Intel**: Manage market intelligence content and insights, including **Spend Insights** (**Awards**, **Solicitations**, **Contracts**, and **Vehicles**) and **Entities** (**Vendors**, **Agencies**, **Offices**, and **People**).
- **Lead Prospector**: Manage prospecting content and lead opportunities, including **Lead Workspace**, **Find & Nurture**, **Procurement Notices**, **SAM.GOV Leads**, and **Re-Competes**.
