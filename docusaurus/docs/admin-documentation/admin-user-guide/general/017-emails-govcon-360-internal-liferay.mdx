Use the **Emails** page to draft and send messages directly from the portal. You can reach this page through the main application navigation.

![Emails](/img/admin/017-emails-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Compose Email

The **Compose Email** form includes standard header fields and a rich text editing toolbar to format your message.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **From** | Text | No | Displays the sender email address (`email-sender-from`). |
| **Enter recipient email addresses** | Text | No | Enter one or more destination email addresses. Use the **CC** or **BCC** options as needed. |
| **Enter a subject** | Text | No | Enter the subject line for your message. |
| **Message** | Textarea | No | Rich text body field for your email content. |

## Formatting Toolbar

The message editor includes formatting tools to customize your text and layout:

- **Text Color** and **Background Color**
- **Bold (Ctrl+B)**, **Italic (Ctrl+I)**, **Underline (Ctrl+U)**, and **Strikethrough**
- **Size** adjustment dropdown
- **Align Left**, **Center**, and **Align Right** alignment options
- **Insert/Remove Numbered List** and **Insert/Remove Bulleted List**

Use **Attach** to add files, then click **Send Email** to dispatch your message or **Cancel** to discard your draft.
