Manage invoices, track payment status, and keep all billing information organized in one place for B2B customers. Access this view by navigating to **Commerce** → **Invoices and Payment**.

![Invoices And Payment](/img/admin/012-invoices-and-payment-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Summary cards

The top section provides high-level metrics for your billing operations:

* **Outstanding**: Displays the total monetary amount of pending or unpaid invoices.
* **Overdue**: Shows the count of invoices that have passed their payment due date.
* **Draft**: Indicates the number of invoices currently saved as drafts.

## Search and filters

Use the filter and search controls above the invoice table to locate specific records quickly.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search by recipient name or invoice numnber** | Text | No | Enter keywords to filter the table by customer name or specific invoice identifiers. |
| **All Status** | Select-one | No | Filter invoices by their current processing or payment status. |
| **All Payment Method** | Select-one | No | Filter invoices by the method of payment used (e.g., ACH). |

## Invoice management

Click **Upload Invoice** to add and register new billing documents into the system. The main table displays details such as recipient **Name**, **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date**, **Due date**, **Billing piriod**, **Amount**, **Payment Method**, and **Status**. Use the row-level action icons to view or download specific invoice records.
