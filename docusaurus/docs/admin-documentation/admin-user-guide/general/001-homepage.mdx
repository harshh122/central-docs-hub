Access the GovCon360 platform by signing in with your registered credentials to manage government contracts, track pursuits, and discover new opportunities. 

![Homepage](/img/admin/001-homepage-screenshot.png)

## Sign in to your account

Use the authentication panel on the right side of the screen to access your workspace.

1. Navigate to the login page and ensure the **Log in** tab is selected.
2. Enter your account credentials into the form fields:

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enter email** | Email | Yes | Enter the email address associated with your account. |
| **Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your account password. |
| **Remember Me** | Checkbox | No | Select to keep your session active on trusted devices. |

3. Click **Sign In** to access your account. After you sign in, you land on your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx).

## Additional options

If you need assistance accessing your account or wish to register a new profile, use the following options on the login interface:

- **Forgot Password**: Click this link beneath the password field to reset your credentials.
- **Sign Up**: Switch to the **Sign Up** tab at the top of the authentication card if you need to create a new profile.
