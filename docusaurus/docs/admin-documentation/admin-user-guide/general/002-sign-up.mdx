Use the Sign Up page to create a new GovCon360 account and access platform features. If you already have an existing account, you can switch to the sign-in view or return to the [Sign In](./001-sign-in.mdx) page.

![Sign Up](/img/admin/002-sign-up-screenshot.png)

## Sign up for an account

Complete the registration form to create your account and get started.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enter First Name** | text | No | Your given name. |
| **Enter Last Name** | text | No | Your family name. |
| **Enter email** | email | No | Your primary email address for notifications and account access. |

1. Enter your details into the **Enter First Name**, **Enter Last Name**, and **Enter email** fields.
2. Click **Get Started** to submit your information and create your account.
3. Alternatively, click **NL Identity** to authenticate using your organizational identity provider.

If you already have an account, click the **Log in** button at the top of the form or the **Log in** text link to open the login view.
