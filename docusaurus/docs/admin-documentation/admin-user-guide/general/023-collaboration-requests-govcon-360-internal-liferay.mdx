Use the **Collaboration Requests** page to submit and manage partnership inquiries or support messages. Access this page from the top navigation bar to view the general information form.

## General Information

Complete the required contact and organization details to submit your collaboration request. 

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Name** | Text | No | Enter your given name. |
| **Last Name** | Text | No | Enter your surname. |
| **Company Name** | Text | No | Enter the name of your organization or company. |
| **Email Address** | Text | No | Enter your preferred contact email address. |
| **Phone Number** | Text | No | Enter a valid telephone number for follow-up communications. |
| **Message** | Textarea | No | Enter your inquiry, notes, or details regarding the collaboration request (up to 1000 characters). |
