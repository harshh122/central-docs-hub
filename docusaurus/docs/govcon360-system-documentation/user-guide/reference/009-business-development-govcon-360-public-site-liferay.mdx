The **Business Development** section acts as a clear action plan to guide you seamlessly through every stage of pursuing a contract, organizing essential agencies and vendors, and turning a complicated process into a confident strategy.

## Federal Opportunity Pipeline

When you are ready to pursue a contract, it moves to your **Opportunities** board. This visual layout lets you see where every potential deal stands. You can click any opportunity card to open a detailed view containing your notes, documents, and team collaboration tools, and drag and drop cards through four clear stages.
