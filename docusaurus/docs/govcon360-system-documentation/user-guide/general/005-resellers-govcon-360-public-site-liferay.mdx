Explore GovCon360's specialized solutions for resellers to match specific catalogs with the right agency buyers at the right time. You can access this information by navigating to **Solutions For** and selecting the relevant reseller option from the public site navigation.

## Header elements

The top navigation bar provides quick access to platform information and account actions:

| Element | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GovCon360** logo | Returns you to the main home page. |
| **Home** | Navigates back to the landing page. |
| **Solutions For** | Opens a dropdown menu displaying targeted industry and business type solutions. |
| **Features** | Displays core capabilities and platform tooling. |
| **Pricing** | Outlines subscription plans and pricing structures. |
| **Resources** | Provides access to helpful guides, documentation, and materials. |
| **About** | Shares company background and mission information. |
| **Explore** | Lets you browse additional platform offerings. |
| **Schedule a Demo** | Opens a request form to schedule a live product demonstration. |
| **Login** | Directs existing users to the sign-in portal. |

## The Challenge of Moving Products at Scale

Reselling commercial hardware and software to the government requires speed and precision. The platform addresses key reseller pain points by focusing on three core areas:

- **Finding Active Buyers**: Search through government procurement data to locate agencies actively buying your specific brands.
- **High Volume Tracking**: Manage hundreds of fast-turnaround quotes without losing track of pipeline momentum.
- **Building Prime Relationships**: Get your catalog in front of the right primes and buyers to secure recurring contracts.

## Managing Cookies and Privacy

When visiting the site, a cookie consent banner appears at the bottom of the screen to manage your preferences:

1. Click **Customize** to adjust granular privacy and tracking settings.
2. Click **Reject all** to decline non-essential cookies.
3. Click **Accept all** to enable personalized content, analytics, and demographic data collection.
