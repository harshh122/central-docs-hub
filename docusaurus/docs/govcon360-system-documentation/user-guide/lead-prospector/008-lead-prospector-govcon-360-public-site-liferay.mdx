GovCon360 Lead Prospector provides simple, configurable searches and market intelligence tools to help you identify winnable federal contracts and agencies that are buying what you sell. Access this page from the public navigation menu by selecting **Features** and then choosing **Lead Prospector**.

![Government Contract Lead Prospector](/img/public/008-lead-prospector-govcon-360-public-site-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Lead Prospector and Market Intel

The top navigation header contains links to explore additional platform capabilities, pricing, resources, and scheduling options. Use the main action buttons in the header, such as **Schedule a Demo** or **Login**, to get started with your account.

## Federal Find & Nurture

Discover leads tailored specifically for your business by setting up preset filters. The **Find & Nurture** tab automatically displays contracts that match your unique capabilities using powerful parameters like **Keywords**, **NAICS**, **PSC**, **Agency**, contract value, and place of performance. 

Review the available FAQ and feature exploration items by interacting with the page buttons, including **What are the federal contract vehicles and procurement systems used?**, **How do I keep track of the leads I find?**, **I am new to government contracting. How do I know which leads are actually meant for my business?**, **How far in advance should I start preparing for re-competes?**, **Why should I use GovCon360's Find & Nurture instead of just searching SAM.gov?**, and **How does Pre-RFP intelligence help in government contracting?**.
