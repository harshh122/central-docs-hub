The Dashboard is your primary landing page upon signing in, giving you an immediate high-level overview of your company profile, active pursuits, and actionable market intelligence. You can access it anytime by selecting **Dashboard** in the main navigation bar.

![Dashboard](/img/client/001-dashboard-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png)

## Header elements

The header area stays fixed across the application, providing system-wide navigation and profile details.

| Element | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dashboard** | Returns you to your main [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx). |
| **Market Intel** | Opens market intelligence tools and signals. |
| **Lead Prospector** | Navigates to [Lead Prospector and Market Intel](./lead-prospector-and-market-intel.mdx). |
| **Business Development** | Manages your pipeline and business development workflows. |
| **Teaming Partnerships** | Explores potential teaming and partnership opportunities. |
| **Proposals** | Manages proposal development and submissions. |
| **Contracts** | Tracks awarded contracts and contract vehicles. |
| **Administration** | Accesses tenant settings, users, and administrative options. |

## Your company

The top-left section displays your organization's core identifier details, CAGE code, UEI, and primary NAICS codes. 

- Select **View GovCon360 Profile** to check your public-facing platform profile.
- Select **Manage Profile** to edit your underlying company details and compliance settings.
- Review your **Website**, **Primary Contact**, **SAM.GOV Status**, and registered **Certifications** directly within the summary cards.

The top-right card indicates your **GovCon360 Company Profile** completion progress. Select **Complete Company Profile** to finish remaining onboarding steps and ensure your business is fully GovCon-ready.

## Lead Prospector

The middle section summarizes your active pipeline and new opportunities pulled into your workspace:

- **New Leads**: Displays leads added to **In Evaluation** in the last 7 days.
- **Active Leads**: Tracks leads currently placed in **Plans to Pursue**.
- **Expiring**: Monitors workspace leads with an action date coming up in the next 180 days.
- **SAM.gov**: Counts new SAM.gov solicitations added in the last 14 days.

## Market Intel

The bottom section surfaces actionable signals, active buying agencies, expiring contracts, matching solicitations, and key agency contacts. Select the arrow icon on any intelligence card to expand and review detailed records.
