Use the Login Page to access GovCon360 by entering your credentials. After you sign in you land on your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx).

![Login Page](/img/client/login-page-screenshot.png)

## Sign in to GovCon360

1. Enter your corporate email address in the **Email Address** field.
2. Enter your password in the **Password** field. Click the **Show password** icon to toggle password visibility.
3. Check **Remember me** if you want your browser to save your session.
4. Click **Sign In**.

## Sign-in options and form fields

| Field / Element | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Email Address** | Text | Yes | Your registered work email address. |
| **Password** | Password | Yes | Your account password. |
| **Remember me** | Checkbox | No | Keeps you logged in across browser sessions. |
| **Forgot Password?** | Link | No | Triggers a password recovery flow if you cannot remember your credentials. |
| **Sign In** | Button | No | Authenticates your credentials and opens your dashboard. |
