description: "Update your account password securely using the Change Password form."

Update your account password securely by navigating to your profile settings and completing the required security fields.

![Reset Password](/img/client/004-reset-password-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png)

## Change Password form

Use the **Change Password** card to update your credentials. Your new password must be different from any previously used passwords.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Current Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your existing account password. |
| **New Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your desired new password. |
| **Reenter Password** | Password | Yes | Re-enter your new password to confirm. |

### Password requirements

Your new password must meet the following criteria, indicated by the checklist below the form:

- Must be at least 8 characters
- Must contain an upper case letter
- Must contain a lower case letter
- Must contain a number
- Must contain a special character

## Actions

1. Enter your current password and your desired new password.
2. Click **Change Password** to save your new credentials, or click **Cancel** to discard your changes and return to your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx).
